FORGE / LEAN PHYSIQUE

Your daily plan

Nutrition rules

  • Protein: 190–210 g daily
  • Fat: about 65–85 g daily; use carbohydrates for the remaining calories
  • Fibre: 25–35 g; eat fruit and vegetables daily
  • Limit alcohol, liquid calories, untracked oils, fried foods and oversized portions
  • Higher-calorie leg days: add carbohydrates around lifting, not unrestricted meals
Targets are starting estimates, not guaranteed deficits. The 2,600-calorie days are a deficit only if your maintenance exceeds 2,600 calories.

Progression

Warm up 5–10 minutes. Keep 1–3 reps in reserve. Increase load when all sets reach the top of their rep range with good form. Rest 2–3 minutes for compound lifts, 60–120 seconds for isolation exercises.

Use weekly average weigh-ins. If weight stays flat for 2–3 weeks, adjust intake by 100–200 calories per day. Prioritize sleep and recovery.
